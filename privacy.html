Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information that customers provide when contacting businesses powered by Burdenless AI, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Service address
  • Nature of service request

How We Use Information

Information collected is used solely to:

  • Connect customers with the service business they contacted
  • Send SMS notifications related to their service request
  • Schedule follow-up communications

SMS Communications

Customers may optionally choose to receive SMS messages from a business powered by Burdenless AI. SMS consent is voluntary and is never required to receive service. Customers who decline SMS still receive full service by phone or email.

For customers who opt in, messages may include appointment confirmations, technician arrival notices, job completion summaries, invoice totals, and follow-up reminders. Message frequency varies based on your service activity. Message and data rates may apply.

No mobile information or messaging consent will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages will be sent. To re-subscribe, reply START. Reply HELP for support contact information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell customer information, and we do not share mobile information or messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information is shared only with the service business the customer contacted, and with Twilio, Inc., our SMS delivery provider, solely to deliver the text messages you requested. Twilio's privacy policy is available at twilio.com/legal/privacy.

Data Retention

Customer information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the service request and any related follow-up communications, or until the customer requests deletion.
