Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information that customers provide when contacting businesses powered by Burdenless AI, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Service address
  • Nature of service request

How We Use Information

Information collected is used solely to:

  • Connect customers with the service business they contacted
  • Send SMS notifications related to their service request, where the customer has opted in
  • Schedule follow-up communications

SMS Communications

Customers may choose to receive SMS notifications related to their service request, including appointment confirmations, technician arrival notices, job completion summaries, invoice totals, and follow-up reminders.

SMS consent is collected through an explicit, optional opt-in: customers sign up via our opt-in form or affirmatively agree when asked during a booking call. Providing a phone number alone does not constitute consent to receive SMS.

Consent is not a condition of purchase or service. Customers who decline SMS receive identical service and are contacted by phone or email instead.

Message frequency varies based on your service activity. Message and data rates may apply.

To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages will be sent. To re-subscribe, reply START. Reply HELP for support contact information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell customer information. No mobile information or text messaging originator opt-in data will be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information is shared only with the service business the customer contacted, and with Twilio, Inc., our SMS delivery provider, solely for the purpose of delivering text messages. Twilio's privacy policy is available at twilio.com/legal/privacy.

Data Retention

Customer information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the service request and any related follow-up communications, or until the customer requests deletion. Opt-in consent records (name, phone number, and timestamp) are retained as proof of consent for as long as the customer remains subscribed.
